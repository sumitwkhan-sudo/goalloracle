How to play

A guided three-step bracket

Rank the four teams in each of the twelve groups (scoring 3 / 2 / 1 / 1 by finishing position, 84 points), select the eight best third-placed teams who advance (2 points each, 16), and fill in your knockout bracket, where later rounds are worth more (up to 109). That's 209 points in total, and it takes about ten minutes. The leaderboard ranks by accuracy, so even a partial bracket counts.

What is a Quick Pick?

Quick Picks is GoalOracle's simple prediction mode. Rank the four teams within each group, choose the eight best third-placed teams, and pick winners through the knockout bracket. The app fills in the rest.

How does scoring work?

You fill out a guided bracket worth 209 points: group rankings score 3 / 2 / 1 / 1 by finishing position (84), each best-third pick is worth 2 (16), and knockout winners are worth more in later rounds — 2 in the Round of 32 up to 12 for the final (109). The leaderboard ranks by accuracy, with ties broken by who submitted first.

How are match results verified?

GoalOracle uses a dual-source oracle. Both football-data.org and api-football.com must agree on the final score before points are awarded. Disputes trigger manual admin review.

When do predictions lock?

Predictions lock five minutes before each match kicks off. You can update your picks as many times as you want before the lock.

What is Annexe C third-place routing?

Annexe C is FIFA's official routing matrix for the 8 best third-placed teams. With 12 groups in the 2026 World Cup, 12 teams finish third; the top 8 advance to the Round of 32. Annexe C determines which 8 advance and which specific bracket slot each one fills — there are 495 possible combinations, all enumerated in the FIFA rulebook. GoalOracle's prediction engine implements all 495 combinations exactly, making it the only public English-language tool that's fully rule-faithful on this.
