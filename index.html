Two ways to play

Quick Picks

A guided three-step wizard: rank the four teams in each of the twelve groups, select the eight best third-placed teams, and fill in your knockout bracket. Takes about ten minutes. Scoring totals 76 points — 36 for group stage, 8 for best thirds, and 32 across the knockouts.

Classic Predictions

Predict the score and result of every one of the 104 matches, from group stage to the final. Three points for a correct result, five points for an exact score, plus bonuses for correct extra time (1) and penalty shootout (2) calls. League creators can customize these point values.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoalOracle free?

Yes. GoalOracle is completely free to play. There are no entry fees, no real-money prizes, and no gambling of any kind.

When does the 2026 World Cup start?

The FIFA World Cup 2026 kicks off on 11 June 2026 at 15:00 ET, hosted across the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The final is on 19 July 2026.

What is a Quick Pick?

Quick Picks is GoalOracle's simple prediction mode. Rank the four teams within each group, choose the eight best third-placed teams, and pick winners through the knockout bracket. The app fills in the rest.

How does scoring work?

Classic Predictions awards 3 points for a correct result, 5 for an exact score, plus 1 for extra time and 2 for penalty calls. Quick Picks totals 76 points across group rankings, best-thirds picks, and knockouts.

How are match results verified?

GoalOracle uses a dual-source oracle. Both football-data.org and api-football.com must agree on the final score before points are awarded. Disputes trigger manual admin review.

Do I need crypto or money to play?

No. GoalOracle is free. Sign up with email, Google, or Twitter/X. An embedded wallet is created for future features but no funds are collected.

When do predictions lock?

Predictions lock five minutes before each match kicks off. You can update your picks as many times as you want before the lock.
